# Brackeys Asset Pack

These are the art, font, sound, and music files used in the Godot tutorial video. They come from Brackeys' project for the video: https://github.com/Brackeys/first-game-in-godot

They are **public domain** — free to use for anything.

## How to use them

1. In Godot, make an **assets** folder in your project's FileSystem panel (the video does this at 3:22).
2. Open this `brackeysAssets` folder on your computer.
3. Select the **fonts**, **music**, **sounds**, and **sprites** folders and drag them into your Godot **assets** folder.

Use these for the tutorial. For your team's game, make your own art and sound.
